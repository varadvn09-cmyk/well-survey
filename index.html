📡 Offline GPS & Data Guide

How to protect your data offline:

  1. Link Your Folder: On the Home tab, click "Link Folder". Once linked, clicking "Save Record" will automatically write a hard backup JSON file to your device.
  2. Restore Data: If your browser crashes or you clear your cache, simply click "Link Folder" again. The app will find your backup file and restore all your records instantly.
  3. Host Locally: Mobile browsers block GPS access for local files. Use an app like "Simple HTTP Server" to host this HTML file locally.
  4. GPS Outdoors: Stand outside with a clear view of the sky. Without internet (A-GPS), a hardware GPS lock takes 1 to 5 minutes.
